Logistics · Dispatch

Waybill & Tracking System

The problem: Dispatch companies log waybills in spreadsheets and coordinate drivers over WhatsApp. Waybills go missing, customers call constantly for status, and fees are calculated by hand.

What I built: A staff dashboard to log a waybill in under 30 seconds, assign a driver, and update status (Pending → Dispatched → In Transit → Delivered). Each status change automatically texts the recipient and sender. Customers track their package on a public page — no login, no phone call.

  • Staff login with session auth
  • Automatic SMS on every status change (Termii)
  • Public tracking timeline per waybill
  • Search by tracking code or phone number

FastAPI · SQLAlchemy · Jinja2 · Termii SMS

Retail · Inventory

Stock & Order Management

The problem: Neighbourhood retailers track stock in a notebook. Reordering is guesswork, sales aren't recorded against stock, and there's no customer history.

What I built: A simple inventory list with low-stock alerts, a point-of-sale screen that decrements stock automatically when a sale is logged, and a printable receipt. Customer phone numbers are saved so you can follow up or text a receipt.

  • Real-time stock counts with low-stock flags
  • Sales automatically reduce stock
  • Customer records and purchase history
  • Optional SMS receipt to the customer

FastAPI · SQLAlchemy · Jinja2 · Termii SMS
